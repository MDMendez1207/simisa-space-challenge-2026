Creado con la herramienta de generación de imágenes integrada, para este prototipo. No representa un paciente ni datos de NASA. La imagen se utiliza con mezcla de pantalla para integrarse al fondo oscuro; no se editaron sus píxeles después de la generación.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-body anatomical illustration for SIMISA interactive body explorer. Create ONE high quality full length human body in front view, perfectly centered, head at top and feet at bottom, arms slightly away from torso with hands visible and legs a little separated. Adult gender neutral medical mannequin without external genital detail. Transparent background, absolutely no UI, labels, text, numbers, circles, floor, shadows or surrounding elements. Style: premium cinematic medical hologram, translucent dark blue glass body with crisp luminous cyan edges, subtle realistic anatomical contours, finely detailed visible skeleton and ribcage, translucent softly cyan lungs, subtle warm coral anatomical heart correctly placed in subject's left chest (viewer right), stylized digestive organs visible in abdomen, faint neural structures in head. Anatomically plausible proportions, elegant rather than scary, no gore. Keep organs integrated in the same body not separate diagrams. Clean silhouette and abundant inner detail so zooming into chest or head remains beautiful. Portrait aspect ratio, maximum useful resolution, entire figure occupies almost full height with 4% margin and centered horizontally. The body will be placed over a very dark navy background. Real transparency outside figure.

## Límites

Ilustración conceptual generada con IA, sin validación anatómica. La navegación consiste en desplazamientos y acercamientos sobre la imagen, no en una reconstrucción volumétrica.
