# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

SIMISA contributors commit to making collaboration respectful, inclusive, and focused. We welcome different backgrounds, disciplines, and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with respect and give actionable feedback.
- Credit the work and sources of others.
- Ask before sharing another contributor's personal information or private work.
- Accept corrections when scientific, technical, or accessibility evidence changes a decision.
- Protect the limited time of the team by keeping work visible and scoped.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption, and unauthorized disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

During the NASA Space Apps Challenge, concerns should be raised with the team lead or the appropriate local-event organizer. Urgent safety concerns should be escalated through the event's official channels.

Project maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when behavior conflicts with this agreement.
